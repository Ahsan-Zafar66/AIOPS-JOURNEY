What an active listener do?
Ask open ended questions
Do not interrupt th speaker or person speaking
Listen with an objective
Learning mindset remains the top priority.

9 steps to follow during active listening:
1) Face the speaker and maintain EYE contact
2) Be attentive
3) Be mentally relaxed
4) Keep an open mind
5) Listen to the words and try to picture what the speaker is saying
6) Don't interrupt and don't impose your solution
7) Wait for the speaker to pause to ask clarifying questions
8) Ask questions only to ensure understanding
9) Try to feel what the speaker is feeling (from empathy point of view)
