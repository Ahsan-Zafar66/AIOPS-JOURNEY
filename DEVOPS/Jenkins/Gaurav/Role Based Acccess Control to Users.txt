Role based access control means give those privileges to a person that are required for their roles.


How to configure Role Based Access in Jenkins?

Step # 1 
Go to plugins and search "role based"

you will see a plugin, install that plugin

Step # 2
GO to manage jenkins
then go to security and change Authorization Strategy
Usualay it is anyone can do anything

Change anyone can do anything to Role-based Strategy


Step # 3
Now go to manage jenkins where you went to security section along security section you will see now another section
which is "Manage and Assign Roles"

Step # 4
Go to manage and assign roles

make a role and assign permissions to it according to your needs
then assign the role to a user

and now you have successfully configured role based access control to a user
