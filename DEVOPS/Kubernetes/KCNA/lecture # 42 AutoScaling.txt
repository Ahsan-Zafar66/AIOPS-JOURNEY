Scaling is of two types horizontal and vertical.
Horizontal: new pods with same specs will be created

Vertical: Pods resources will be increased such as CPU and RAM NOT MEMORY kyn ky memory mn data hai


Horizontal Pod Autoscaler:
make new pods when required and vice verse

Vertical Pod Autoscaler:
jab ham vertical pod autoscaler bnaty hain usmy defing krty hain ky by default tum ny itna CPU ot RAM dena hai.
Agar zyada required hai to ham limit define krty hain ky maximum itny CPU or RAm dy dena zaroorat paray to 

jab maximum limit jo CPU or RAm ki define ho gi pod us sy bhi oper janay lagay ga to POD DESTROY ho jaye ga.
jab pod destroy hota hai to uski jagah new pod ban jata hai kyn ky hamny define kiya hai ky pod mojood hona chahiye default specs ky sth
