SLI/SLO/SLA are things that are defined during development of application
SLI/SLO/SLA:

SLI: Service Level indicator
ky bank ki website khol rha hn kitni latency hai
aisa indeicator jo service ka level btaye.
ham app to bna lety hain, likin jis trhan ki service dy rhi hai usko bhi track krna hota hai

application customer/user centric hoti hai.


SLO service level object, this defines target for SLI
mtlb ky meri bank ki website ki latency < 100ms honi chahiye
ye mera target hai SLI ky lye

SLI mer website availble honi chahiye
SLO meri website 99.9% uptime rhy

SLO ka metric SLI ky liye hai

SLA service level agreement, ye agreement hota hai company or customer ku darmayan ky mn itni service apko dn ga minimum.
SLA mn mention hota hai bakida ky company kitna SLI, SLO dy gi customers ko
For example habib bank apny customers ko khta hai 90% of time apko website up mily gi apky liye avaible go gi
