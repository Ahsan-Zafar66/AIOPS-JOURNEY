What is a job?
jobs are designed to run a containerized task successfully to completion

Types of restart Policies:
1) Always
2) Never
3) On Failure


Difference Between Deployment and Job is that deployment creates replicaset and replicaset create pods BUT job directly create pod.


# some parameters of job
completions: how many times to do the job? aik task kitni bar karna hai?
backoffLimits: 10, mtlb 10 bar try krna hai agar 10 bar mn job na ho to job terminate kr do
activeDeadlineSeconds: 50, mtlb ky 50 second mn job khtm krni hai sari ki sari na ki to job terminate kr do


# vim job.yml
apiVersion: batch/v1 # job is present in this path in kubernetes
kind: Job
metadata:
  name: first-job
spec:
  completions: 5 # task ko 5 bar krna hai
  backoffLimits: 10  # task ko 10 bar try krna hai us sy zyada nhi, agar 10 bar mn 5 bari task na ho to job band kr do
  activeDeadlineSeconds: 100 # 100 second mn 5 bar task krna hai, 100 second mn na kiya to job band kr do
  template: # task krna kiya hai
    spec: # pod ki specifications
      containers:
      - name: busybox
        image: busybox:stable
        command: ["echo","my name is ahsan"]
      restartPolicy: Never # ye pod ki policy hai na ky container ki. isky do option hoty hain aik Never dsra OnFailure.


# oper wali file mn aik job define ki hai, job ye hai ky pod bnana hai usmy busybox image or command run krna hai. Completions 5 hain to yehi kam 5 bar krna hai.
Mtlb 5 pods bnanay hain or ye task perform krna hai or pod shutdown then. Total 10 bari try krna hai, agar 10 bar mn 5 pods ban liye or 100 second mn sara task complete kr liya to job successful.
