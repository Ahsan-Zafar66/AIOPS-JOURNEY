Full CISSP:
a person who has passed the CISSP exam and 5years of experince in two of 8 domains of CISSP

Associate:
A person who has passed the CISSP exam and do not have any experience is called associate. He is given 6 years to get 5 years of experience in any of the two domains of CISSP


Domains of CISSP:

1) Security and Risk Management

2) Asset Security

3) Security Engineering

4) Communication and Network Security

5) Identity and Acess management

6) Security Assessment and Testing

7) Security Operations

8) Software Development Security

Business Continuity Plan:

make a backup of your data at all cost, so if a disaster happens you would be able to continue your business

Most business makes the mistake that they do not have backup and when disaster happen they fail to continue to the business

Every busniess must have a disaster plan to keep the business going

DOMAIN # 2 Asset Security

Any item deemed by a comany to be valuable are assets. It is anything that adds value to the company. Asets are also referred to as resources.
Assets are protected based on their value

Examples:
People, Information, data, Hardware, Systems, Processes, Software.
Functions, Ideas, Brand, Identity, Intellectual Property
