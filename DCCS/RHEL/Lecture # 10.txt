In centos 7,8

Suppose we have a 16gb hardisk it is 16gb to us but to fdisk it will be expressed in terms of cylinders, like 16gb has 1958 cylinders, cylinders refers to the space we can fill

One cylinder size is approimately of 8MB

calculating hardisk size through cuyliners

no. of cylinders = 92567

one cylinder equals to 8MB


total MBS = 92567 * 8 =740,536 MB

GB = 740536 / 1024 = 723GB

It cannot format, or mount a partition

If you need to make a 5th partition, you have to delete one partition from the already made 4 and now you can make any number of partitions in that extended partition.

These 3 linux and one extended partition are made, extended is just for name, you will new partitions in extended partition and it will hold them. Deleting extended partition will remove all the partitions that it is holding
