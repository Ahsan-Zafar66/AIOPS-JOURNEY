Formula to calculate subnet ips:
192.168.1.0/24 and 192.168.1.0/25, how many subnets in 25?
25 -24 = 1, raise the remainder to the power of 2, 2^1 = 2 --> two subnets

192.168.1.0/26 how many subnets?
26-24 =2 , raising remainder to the power of 2, 2^2 =4 --> 4 subnets
