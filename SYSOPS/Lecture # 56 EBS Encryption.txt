Encryption of EBS is done while creating a volume.

Snapshots of encrypted volume are also encrytped and when you make volumes from these encrytpted snshots those volumes are also encrypted


Once a volume is encrypted you cannot uncrypt it, what you can do you can copy data from encrypted volume into an un-encrypted one


We can also encrypt a snapshot while copying it from one region to the other


When you have a encrypted volume attached to an ec2 instance, then data is secure at rest and in transit, because when we transfer data we transfer encrypted data.


Whatsapp secures data with encryption at rest and in transit?
No, whatsapp only secures data in transit with end to end encryption. Data at rest stored in mobile is not secured by whatsapp.


Un-encrypted snapshots can be shared wth other accounts in the same region publically and privately

Encrypted snapshots can be shared only privately NOT publically
Encrypted snapshots can only be shared privately with selected acounts and we need to give acces of the Custom Management Key.
For sharing sanshots, the volume whose snapshot is taken must be encrytoed wth custome management key but not with default key.

If your volume is encrypted with default key you cannot share it with others

Procedre of sharing key:
1) Go to CMK service
2) Select your key
3) Add AWS account ID to which you want to share key


CMK charges us 1$ per month.
You cannot delete directly. We can schedule key deletion minimum 7 days and maximum 30 days
