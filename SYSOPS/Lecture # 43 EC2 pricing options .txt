On demand : you pay per hour for number of hours you used the service

reserved: you ask AWS to reserve something for you for a long time like a year, three years or more. 
For reserved and long term AWS also gives you a discount.

Different regions have different prices for same ec2 instance.

Production severs run every day 24/7, so production servers are made in reserved category.


When we reserve an instance family, at the backend it is reserved.

Once we reserved a family for a 12 month period and pay it's price our time has started and from that momen till next 12 month time is there to use it



If you reserve instance in  aparticular Availability Zone, than instanc eis reserved for that AZ only, if you run an instance in any other AZ then you will be charged on demand not reserved

If you reserve instance in a availability zone, then your capacity is guarrented in that availability zone

Keep your application and database server in same availabilty zone, reserve them in same availability zone to avoid any future errors if amazon runs out of capacity in that availability zone


If your company wants to have servers in SEOUL region than reserve instances withou specifying availability zone.



If your company says they don;t care about availaibility zones BUT the servers should be in SEOUL region, then you can reserve an instance without specifying availabilty zone and later you can use any avaialbility zone to use that reserved instance.

Suppose you reserved t3.x2large instance with 32 gb memory and 8 cores in SEOUL region, you can break down this reserved instance into two instance havin 4 cores and 16gb memory each and launch them in any availability zone in the SEOUL region

You can sell your reserved instance on the AWS, the buyer will contact you to purchase your reserved instances

After you purchased reserved instances AWS will not refund, you can sell it to some other person, STANDARD can be sold to other customers, If you have chosed CONVERTIBLE you can exchange with AWS

Standard --> means fixed cannot be modifed, have more discount because it has less features, you cannot change family of instance. You can only change Availability zone. Can only be sell to other customers, AWS will take 12% commision of the sale
Standard is dangerous as it does not offer modification, reduce price benefit, you have to sell it own your own

It is advised to use convertible for productions not standard. Because it offers price reduction over time and have benefits of modification

Convertible --> means can be modified, it has less discount as it has more features than standard. It can be exchange for some other instance with AWS. It has price reduction benefit over the period of time. If you payed 600$ first year, and price reduce to 400$ you will pay 400$ for next year

When you reboot, billing hour and ip is not lost. Amazon knows that this person will come again and ip is not lost. And billing hour is also not lost
