Implied Router:
VPC has a feeature called implied router.
no request has to be made, automatic facility.

Implied router automatically does communication between subnets & outside internet world

Suppose your region is Canada and it has two availability zones or data centres

Let's suppose you have two subnets s1 and s2 in az1 and az2.

How these two subnets s1 present in az1 and s2 present in az2 will communicated with each other?
The answer is implied router, they will communicate through implied router.


what if s1 present in az1 needs to communicate outside internet world how it will do?
Through implied router an ec2 intance using ip of s1 and present in az1 will us eimplied router to communicate with outside world.


We do not need to do configurations in implied router, it is automatically present in route tables. Implied router has route tables that have configuration

implied router has route tables which have your server information and allows the outside world to communicate with your server.
